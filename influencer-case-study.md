## 3. Vyapar

**Category:** Built for MSMEs
**Tags:** The Case | Built for MSMEs | Influencer Strategy

> **150+ creators powering business growth for Vyapar**
> Creator → Content → Audience → Lead

### Turning Creator Reach into Real Business Growth.

**The Brief**
Vyapar wanted to amplify its brand voice while turning creator-led content into qualified leads. With previous mega-influencer campaigns delivering poor ROI, the challenge was to find a more targeted way to reach India's MSME owners.

**What We Did**
We shifted the strategy towards regional nano creators whose audiences closely matched Vyapar's core customers, driving stronger relevance and ROI. The approach has evolved across 4 consecutive cohorts, turning into an ongoing monthly partnership with the brand.

*We don't just run campaigns. We build partnerships that last.*

| 150+ | 5.7M+ | 4.85% | 17K+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano Creators Activated | Total Views Generated | Overall Engagement Rate | Leads Generated |

### Creator Mix (cover)

Creator segments featured: E-commerce Sellers, Clothing Business Owners, Business Infotainment Creators, Business Creators, CA & Accounting Creators, Kirana Store Owners, Pharmacy Owners, Regional Creators.

### Cohort Summary

| Cohort | Month | number of Creators | Total Views | Engagement Rate | Leads |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cohort 1 | May | 30 | 650K+ | 4.15% | 2100+ |
| Cohort 2 | June | 35 | 1.1M+ | 5.29% | 3800+ |
| Cohort 3 | July | 45 | 1.7M+ | 4.89% | 5600+ |
| Cohort 4 | August | 50 | 2.2M+ | 5.15% | 6300+ |

### Cohort 1 (May): Test → Expand → Convert

**Our Strategy**
1. **Creator Experiment:** Business, CA, accounting & shop-owner profiles.
2. **Regional Expansion:** Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada & North creators.
3. **Organic Lead Generation:** Native Vyapar integrations built to drive leads.

Results: 30 nano creators activated | 650K+ total views | 4.15% engagement rate | 2100+ leads generated.

### Cohort 2 (June)

**Our Strategy**
1. **Refining the Creator Mix:** More focus on infotainment & CA/accounting creators.
2. **Stronger Script Integration:** Sharper, more natural Vyapar integrations.
3. **Scaling Nano Creators:** Leveraged high-engagement nano creators for stronger results.

Results: 35 nano creators activated | 1.1M+ total views | 5.29% engagement rate | 3800+ leads generated.

### Cohort 3 (July)

**Our Strategy**
1. **Deepening the Creator Strategy:** Built on the winning creator mix from previous cohorts.
2. **Real Business Stories:** Focused on startup owners, e-commerce sellers & business creators.
3. **Show, Don't Tell:** Creators showcased how they use Vyapar to run and grow their businesses.

Results: 45 nano creators activated | 1.7M+ total views | 4.89% engagement rate | 5600+ leads generated.

### Cohort 4 (August)

**Our Strategy**
1. **Business-Owner First:** Focused heavily on creators running real businesses.
2. **Real-World Business Stories:** E-commerce, clothing, kirana & pharmacy owners.
3. **Reaching the Right Audience:** Tapped Tier 2 & 3 audiences, driving massive lead generation.

Results: 50 nano creators activated | 2.2M+ total views | 5.15% engagement rate | 6300+ leads generated.

## 4. Polaris School of Technology

**Category:** Ed-Tech School
**Tags:** The Case | Ed-Tech School | Work Highlights

> **Turning curiosity into PSAT leads.**
> Creator-led lead generation campaign.


### The Power of Blind Marketing.

**The Brief**
Polaris wanted to generate maximum potential leads for its PSAT entrance exam during the admission season. The challenge was to create enough curiosity and hype to make students actively explore the opportunity.

**What We Did**
We took a blind-marketing approach: no tags, no collab posts and not even a single mention of Polaris. By creating curiosity-led content that felt completely organic, we turned creator videos into high-engagement conversations, generating massive leads for the PSAT.
